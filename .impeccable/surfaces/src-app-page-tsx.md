## Scope

Homepage (`/`), assessment start, quiz, finish sequence, free result and parent unlock (`/assessment`). Homepage mode: Persuade. Quiz mode: Operate, for a child aged 9-12 with a parent beside them.

## Audience and action

Parent on a mid-range Android phone deciding a CBC pathway. One action: "Take the Test". All copy comes from the live ramani.app page; only typo, "&amp;" and dash fixes. Proof: 200+ schools, 50K+ assessments, 4.9★, 1,089+ students, UniConnect Centre, BridgeTalent, Caroline Mutoko video, three testimonials.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard for a free personality-style test, built at the polish of 16Personalities, Duolingo, Khan Academy and Brilliant. Refuses the live page's gradient hero, cutout-in-a-circle and icon bubbles, and refuses pale all-white pages and decorative lane lines.

OWN-WORLD: Committed colour. Whole sections are solid brand fields: blue #2563EB, navy #1B294B, amber #F59E0B, with a pale blue tint only behind white cards. Soft curved edges where fields meet, no rules. Chunky rounded buttons with a solid bottom edge. Ramani's own photos run large. Instrument Sans, condensed display.

STORY: The parent sees the offer in one line, tries a real question in the hero, picks a grade, sees how it works in Ramani's own pictures, watches Caroline Mutoko, trusts partners and schools, taps Take the Test.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Blue field. Left: eyebrow, headline "Help every student find their path", the live subline, amber Take the Test button, "free, no account" note. Right: a live question card with the real question 24 activities; tapping one answers it and points to the test. Stats on an amber band below.

FORM: Category standard (canon), chosen on the decision page; seed:37e74e1e500b9cfb.

SIGNATURE: The hero question card is a working sample of the quiz.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
