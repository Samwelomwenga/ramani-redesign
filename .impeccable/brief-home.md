## Scope

Homepage (`/`), assessment start, quiz, finish sequence, free result and parent unlock (`/assessment`). Homepage mode: Persuade. Quiz mode: Operate, for a child aged 9-12 with a parent beside them.

## Audience and action

Parent on a mid-range Android phone deciding a CBC pathway. One action everywhere: "Start the free assessment". Proof on hand: 200+ schools, 50K+ assessments, 4.9★, 1,088+ students, UniConnect Centre, BridgeTalent, Caroline Mutoko, three learner testimonials. No invented guarantees or stats.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every child belongs to a house; Ramani names it and shows the pathway it leads to. Refuses the edtech default of a gradient hero, cutout student photo and icon bubbles.

OWN-WORLD: A Kenyan school sports day. Navy field regions marked with white chalk lane lines. Six house colours (blue, amber, rose, emerald, teal, violet), one per RIASEC interest, appear as lane stripes, sashes and felt rosettes, never ranked. Cool light ground between fields. Bunting pennants as section markers. Buttons are blue "starter" tabs; chips are sash stripes.

STORY: The parent sees what Ramani does in one line, believes it is built for CBC and Kenyan careers, sees the price and what the child gets, trusts the partners, and taps start. The child runs four relay legs and finishes holding a rosette.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Navy field fills the hero. Six chalked lanes run left to right behind the copy, each tinted with its house colour at the start line. Left: headline, one-line how, the blue CTA on the start line with "About 12 minutes · Free to start" under it, proof line. Right: a sample result rosette card ("Amani, Grade 6 · House of Makers") labelled sample. Mobile: copy first, rosette card below, lanes compress to a stripe band.
